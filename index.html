Spanish Creative Writing Competition for High School Students

High school juniors and seniors are encouraged to submit their original creative writing works in Spanish!

Theme: "Spanish in My Life / El español en mi vida"
🏆
Awards & Campus Dinner: Awards will be presented during a special dinner on the Odessa campus.

Theme & Areas of Interest

We invite high school juniors and seniors—whether native speakers, heritage speakers, or Spanish learners—to submit original, unpublished creative works written in Spanish centered on the theme "Spanish in My Life / El español en mi vida." Areas of particular interest include, but are not limited to:

Short Stories

Fictional narratives inspired by bilingual experiences, family traditions, or moments where the Spanish language plays a meaningful role.

Creative Essays & Chronicles (crónicas)

Non-fiction reflections, memoirs, or journalistic pieces about growing up with Spanish, learning the language, or navigating two cultures.

Poetry

Individual poems or a collection of poems expressing emotions, memories, or cultural identity tied to the Spanish language.

Submission Guidelines

📄 Accepted Forms

Short stories, poetry, creative essays, and chronicles (crónicas).

🌐 Language

Submissions must be written in Spanish.

📝 Formatting

Double-spaced text using a 12-point font.

✨ Originality

Works must not have been published previously.

📏 Length

Poetry: 1 to 10 pages.
Prose: 2 to 10 pages.
