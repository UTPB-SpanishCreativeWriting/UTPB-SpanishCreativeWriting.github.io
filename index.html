Spanish Creative Writing Competition for High School Students

High school juniors and seniors are encouraged to submit their original creative writing works in Spanish!

Theme: "Spanish in My Life / El español en mi vida"
🏆
Awards & Campus Dinner: Awards will be presented during a special dinner on the Odessa campus.

Theme & Areas of Interest

We invite high school juniors and seniors, whether Spanish learners, heritage speakers, or native speakers, to submit original, unpublished creative works written entirely in Spanish centered on the theme "Spanish in My Life / El español en mi vida." Areas of particular interest include, but are not limited to:

Short Stories

Fictional narratives inspired by growing up between two cultures, family traditions, or moments where the Spanish language plays a meaningful role.

Chronicles (crónicas)

Personal reflections, memoirs, or true stories about growing up with Spanish or learning the language.

Poetry

Individual poems or a collection of poems expressing emotions, memories, or cultural identity tied to the Spanish language.

Submission Guidelines

📄 Accepted Forms

Short stories, poetry, and chronicles (crónicas).

🌐 Language

Submissions must be written entirely in Spanish.

📝 Formatting

Double-spaced text using a 12-point font.

✨ Originality

Works must not have been published previously.

📏 Length

Poetry: Minimum 1 page / Maximum 10 pages.
Prose: Minimum 2 pages / Maximum 10 pages.
