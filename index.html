Spanish Creative Writing Competition for High School Students

High school juniors and seniors are encouraged to submit their original creative writing works in Spanish!
🏆
Awards & Campus Dinner: Awards will be presented during a special dinner on the Odessa campus.
Submission Guidelines
📝 Accepted Forms
Short stories, poetry, and creative essays.
🌐 Language
Submissions must be written in Spanish.
📄 Formatting
Double-spaced text using a 12-point font.
✨ Originality
Works must not have been published previously.
📏 Length
Between 2 pages minimum and 10 pages maximum.
Important Dates
Submissions Due
April 18, 2027
Finalists Notification
April 28, 2027
